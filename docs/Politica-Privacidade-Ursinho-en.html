Privacy policy

Português · English · Español

Updated November 18, 2026

Ursinho Amigão is an offline educational game with no user account, advertising or tracking tools. This policy applies to the Android app.

Progress and settings

Saved progress, language, accessibility settings and volume levels stay in private app storage on your device until replaced, cleared in Android settings or removed by uninstalling.

Name on the diploma

A first name is optional and personalizes the diploma on your device. It is not sent to the developer. It may appear in a file you choose to save or share.

Files and sharing

Diplomas and coloring materials are exported at your request. Android lets you choose where to save or share them. The receiving app has its own privacy practices. Copies saved outside the game remain until you delete them.

Temporary copies

Exports are prepared in private app cache. Copies older than 24 hours are removed the next time the app opens or before another export. Android may clear the cache sooner.

Children and caregivers

The game does not request full names, addresses, location or contacts. A caregiver can help a child save or share a diploma containing their first name.

Delete data

In Android settings, open Apps, Ursinho Amigão, Storage and clear data to remove progress and settings, or clear cache to remove temporary copies. Delete downloaded or shared files from their respective locations.
